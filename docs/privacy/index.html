1. Scope, publisher, and roles

7thcode is the publisher name used for software and documentation. The contracting provider for an app is the legal entity or individual identified in the applicable Atlassian Marketplace listing, order, or product-specific terms. This policy does not change that identity.

This policy explains how the applicable provider handles information through this site, support communications, and its apps. Atlassian separately controls information it processes under its own agreements and privacy policy. A customer organization normally controls the Jira information its users direct an app to process.

2. Website and support information

This policy site does not provide user accounts, advertising, or 7thcode-operated analytics. 7thcode does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies on this site. GitHub may process ordinary request metadata, such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, and timestamps, to deliver, secure, and operate GitHub Pages under GitHub’s own terms.

When you contact support, we process the contact details, message, diagnostic identifiers, and other information you choose to provide to respond, investigate, secure the products, prevent abuse, and maintain a proportionate support record. Depending on the context, processing is necessary to perform a contract, comply with law, or pursue legitimate interests in support and security. Please do not send customer content, account IDs, or credentials unless necessary and we have agreed on an appropriate method.

3. Marketplace apps

Each app accesses only the Atlassian data and permissions described in its Marketplace listing and product documentation. 7thcode does not sell End-User Data, disclose it to data brokers, or use it for targeted advertising. Product-specific hosting, retention, deletion, permission, and service-provider information takes precedence over a general description on this page.

4. CloneGuard for Jira

HostingAtlassian Forge
External app egressNone declared
Operational retentionUp to 365 days
Advertising / saleNone

At a user’s request, CloneGuard accesses Jira work item fields, hierarchy, comments, attachments, links, project and create-screen metadata, user references required for mapping, and permission results. It uses this information to prepare a preflight contract and create only the approved destination items.

CloneGuard stores minimized operational records in installation-scoped Forge storage: Atlassian account identifiers, source and target item identifiers, mappings, fingerprints, approved field recipes, job and artifact states, safe error codes, templates, and source-to-target results. It does not store Jira descriptions, comment bodies, attachment names or bytes, passwords, API tokens, or secrets in job history or application logs.

Records expire after 365 days unless erased earlier. Forge privacy events are used to erase records linked to a closed Atlassian account. After uninstall, Atlassian may retain soft-deleted Forge-hosted data under its platform recovery lifecycle. CloneGuard contains no Jira delete path and does not modify source work items.

5. Flow Guard and StatusLens

Flow Guard’s product policy describes Forge-hosted compute and storage, no declared app-specific external data egress, minimized risk records, product-specific retention, and administrator export and deletion controls. StatusLens documentation describes Forge-only processing, per-project filter settings, a 15-minute aggregate cache, and no stored issue content. Those product documents control where they are more specific.

6. Sharing, transfers, and service providers

7thcode does not share app data with advertisers or data brokers. Atlassian provides the Marketplace, Jira, and Forge platform. GitHub provides this policy site, and the email provider processes information needed for support communications. These providers may process data in locations permitted by their terms and applicable law. We may disclose limited information when required by law, to protect rights or security, or as part of a lawful business transfer, subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention and deletion

App data follows each product’s stated retention and deletion rules. Support information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the request, security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or a related dispute, and is then deleted or anonymized where reasonably practicable. Backup, platform recovery, and legal-hold copies may remain for a limited period under the relevant provider’s lifecycle.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information. You may also contact the administrator of the Atlassian site that controls your Jira account and content. We may verify a request proportionately, may need to preserve information required by law or security, and will respond as required by applicable law.

9. Security and policy changes

We use data minimization, access controls, content-free diagnostics, and platform security controls appropriate to each app. No system, transmission, or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. See the Security Policy for reporting instructions. Material policy changes will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date and communicated where required.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact 7thcode at support@7thcode.app and identify the affected product. Do not include credentials or confidential Jira content in the first message.
