Product privacy notice

Modorika

What Modorika, the returns and exchanges app for NEXT ENGINE, accesses, stores and transmits, and how it is deleted.

Effective 18 October 2026 · Publisher: 7thcode

1. Scope and provider

This notice applies to the NEXT ENGINE app “Modorika – 返品・交換セルフ管理” (“Modorika”) and its request page for buyers. Modorika is provided by 7thcode (a trade name). It supplements the 7thcode Privacy Policy, and product-specific statements here take precedence.

Modorika has two kinds of users: “shops”, which subscribe to NEXT ENGINE and accept returns and exchanges through Modorika (including their staff), and “buyers”, who bought from a shop and submit a return or exchange request. Buyer information is the shop’s customer information, and the shop decides why it is used. Modorika processes buyer information on the shop’s behalf.

2. Data Modorika accesses

Shops. When Modorika is opened from NEXT ENGINE, it receives the company identifier and name, and the identifier and name of the staff member who opened it. Within the permissions the shop grants in NEXT ENGINE, it reads the shop list, orders (order number, lines, dates, shipping status, and the buyer’s name, address, phone number and email address), products and stock, stock movement history (to confirm a restock), bulk order-registration patterns, payment and delivery method settings, the processing status of uploaded orders, and the company’s address and contact details.

The buyer’s phone number and postal code are used only to verify the buyer on the request page and are not stored. The names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses of the buyer and the recipient are read from the original order when the shop has set up automatic exchange orders and approves an exchange, to create the replacement order in NEXT ENGINE, and are passed straight to NEXT ENGINE without being stored in Modorika.

Buyers. Modorika receives what the buyer enters on the request page (order number, phone number or postal code for verification, return or exchange, items and quantities, reason, item condition, description, and a note to the shop), an optional email address, and optional photos. Photos are resized on the buyer’s device, and metadata such as location (EXIF) is removed on the server before storage.

Access records. To prevent brute-force verification, verification attempts are recorded as a hash tied to the order. The hosting provider may process connection logs, including IP addresses and access times, for normal operation.

3. Data Modorika stores

  • Companies and shops: company identifier and name, shop name, return address, contact email address, settings, the NEXT ENGINE access token (encrypted) and the identifier of the staff member who connected it
  • Rules: return and exchange rules, their versions and who saved them
  • Requests: receipt number, order number, a copy of the order lines and dates (without the buyer’s contact details), reason, condition, description, notes, the buyer’s email address (if entered), photos, inspection results and notes, refund amount and record, return and exchange tracking numbers, the exchange order number, and the status-page key (a hash for matching and an encrypted value for including it in emails)
  • History and processing: request history (including the staff member who acted), records of registrations sent to NEXT ENGINE, emails sent (recipient, subject and body), and AI analysis records (the text sent and the result, described below)
  • Other: personal links issued by the shop (as hashes), verification attempts (as hashes), and monthly request counts and NEXT ENGINE API call counts

4. Purpose

Data is used only to provide the requested features (accepting requests, applying the shop’s rules, emailing buyers and shops, creating exchange orders and updating stock in NEXT ENGINE, analytics and CSV export), to run the AI analysis where the shop enables it, to prevent abuse, to count requests for billing, to investigate failures, and to meet legal obligations. Buyer information is not sold, disclosed to data brokers, or used for targeted advertising.

5. Providers and data location

Modorika runs on the cloud of Railway Corporation (United States), and its data, including photos, is stored in a database on servers in Singapore.

The following providers process limited information as needed to provide the service.

  • NE株式会社 — NEXT ENGINE (reading orders, products and stock; registering exchange orders and stock; billing the app fee)
  • Resend — email to buyers and shops (recipient address, subject and body)
  • OpenAI — AI analysis, only where the shop enables it (see below)
  • Railway Corporation — application and database hosting (servers in Singapore)
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — DNS

These providers may process data in the regions permitted by their own terms and applicable law.

6. AI analysis

AI analysis is enabled by the shop per shop and is off by default. Where it is on, each new request, buyer reply or added photo sends the request content to the OpenAI API and returns reference information for staff, such as a suggested action and points to check. Staff make every approval or rejection, and the AI never contacts buyers.

  • Sent: return or exchange, item names, quantities and unit prices, replacement item names, reason, condition, description, the note to the shop, messages between the shop and the buyer, a summary of the shop’s rules and the rule-based result, days elapsed since the order, shipment and request, and up to four of the most recent photos
  • Not sent: order number, receipt number and the buyer’s email address. Phone numbers, email addresses, postal codes and numbers of ten or more digits in free text are removed before sending. Names written in free text, or information visible in photos, may remain
  • Records: the text sent (after the removal above) and the result are stored with each request
  • At OpenAI: according to OpenAI’s published information, data sent to the API is not used to train OpenAI’s models and is retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. See OpenAI’s policies

7. Transfers to providers abroad

Railway (application and database hosting), OpenAI (AI analysis) and Resend (email) are based in the United States. The database is stored on servers in Singapore. These providers handle information on Modorika’s behalf only as needed to provide the service. For the personal-information protection systems of the United States and Singapore, see the information on foreign systems published by the Personal Information Protection Commission of Japan. Each provider publishes the measures it takes in its own policies.

8. External transmission

Modorika’s buyer request page, shop admin screens and product page do not use tags that send information from the user’s device to third parties, such as analytics or advertising tags. Fonts and images are also served from Modorika’s own server.

9. Retention and deletion

Photos not used in a submitted request are deleted automatically 24 hours after upload. Requests, photos, history, sent emails and AI analysis records are kept while the shop continues to use Modorika. When a shop stops using Modorika in NEXT ENGINE, its data, including photos, AI analysis records and sent emails, is deleted one month after the day it stopped. Copies in backups expire under the hosting provider’s retention period.

10. Individual rights and requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information. Buyers should contact the shop they bought from first, since the shop is the party accepting the request. Shops, and anyone asking about Modorika’s own processing, can write to the address below, naming the receipt number concerned. Requests are reviewed on Japan business days, 10:00–17:00 JST.
